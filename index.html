📖 Game Rules

🎮 How the Game Works

Each round, every creature goes through these 7 phases, always in this order:

  1. 1. Movement - Creatures step toward what they want (food, prey) or run away
  2. 2. Combat - Wolves bite humans, dogs bite wolves, males fight each other
  3. 3. Eating - Humans eat ripe fruits and mushrooms next to them; dogs eat mushrooms
  4. 4. Reproduction - A male next to a female may make her pregnant
  5. 5. Death - Creatures with health ≤ 0 are removed; others may die of old age
  6. 6. Birth - Pregnant females whose time is up give birth
  7. 7. Spawn - New plants and animals appear on empty cells

⚡ Important Rules

  • Every creature finishes a phase before the next phase begins.
  • Creatures get older every round, and the older they are, the more likely they die of old age.
  • Only one creature or plant can be on a cell at a time.
  • 👨 vs 👨: when two males fight, the winner gains the loser’s health as a prize. If both would drop to 0, only the weaker one dies.
  • Overcrowding: with more than 100 humans, every human is 2× more likely to die of old age. With more than 50 wolves + dogs, they are 2× more likely.
  • If a mother has no empty cell next to her when her baby is due, she dies and the baby takes her place.

🔁 Why the Order Matters

Because phases run one after another, an early phase can change what happens later:

  • A 👨 male killed in combat in phase 2 cannot father a child in phase 4 — even if a female is right next to him.
  • A human who drops to 0 health from a wolf bite in phase 2 is too weak to eat in phase 3, so a ripe fruit next to them cannot save them.
  • A pregnant female killed by a wolf is removed in phase 5, before births in phase 6 — so her baby is never born.

🏁 How the Game Ends

The game ends when all humans have died. Humans win when no wolves and no mushrooms are left (checked from round 10 on). You can also press Finish Game at any time to see the summary.

👨 Male Humans

Males walk toward fruits and bite back when a wolf attacks them. Two males next to each other fight. A male next to a female can make her pregnant.

👩 Female Humans

Females walk toward fruits and can become pregnant (🤰). A pregnancy lasts 3 rounds; after giving birth she rests for 2 rounds before she can be pregnant again. Females do not fight back against wolves.

🐺 Wolves

Wolves hunt humans and bite them when they are next to them. They can see farther than humans. Males bite back, females do not.

🐕 Dogs

Dogs protect humans by hunting wolves and biting them. Dogs are immune to mushrooms: they can eat them without getting hurt, which helps clear the poison away.

🍎 Fruits

Fruits heal humans who eat them. A new fruit is unripe (🍏) and becomes ripe (🍎) after 2 rounds. Only ripe fruits can be eaten, and only by a human who is not at full health.

🍄 Mushrooms

Mushrooms are poisonous: a human who eats one loses health. They are dangerous from the moment they appear. Dogs can eat mushrooms safely.

⚠️ Warning: Mushrooms reduce health!

🧬 Genes

Every human has their own genes. A baby gets a mix of both parents’ genes plus a small random change (mutation). Over many rounds, genes that help survival become more common.

  • Max Health - how much health the human can have (and starts with).
  • Strength - how hard the human hits in fights with other males and wolves.
  • Metabolism - how much health every step costs. A fast metabolism means getting hungry quickly.
  • Greed - how likely the human is to walk toward a fruit they can see.
  • Caution - how likely the human is to run away from a wolf they can see.
  • Longevity - how slowly the human ages. Higher longevity means a longer life on average.

⚡ Energy

Health is also energy. How full a human is changes how they behave:

  • Desperate (below 30%): hunts for food hard and does not try to have children. A desperate male runs away from other males.
  • Hungry (below 50%): looks for food more often, is more careful around wolves, and does not try to have children.
  • Moderate (50–80%): normal behaviour.
  • Full (above 80%): less interested in food, braver, and more likely to have children.
  • Satiated (100%): barely interested in food and most likely to have children.

Tip: hover over a human on the board to see their genes and energy state.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space - Pause/Play toggle
  • Right Arrow - Advance 1 round (when paused)
  • Up Arrow - Advance 5 rounds (when paused)
  • Down Arrow - Pause simulation
  • Left Arrow - Pause simulation

🖱️ Mouse Controls
