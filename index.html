📖 Game Rules & Reference

🎮 How the Game Works

This simulator shows how populations interact in an ecosystem. Each round, all creatures follow these phases in order:

  1. Movement - Creatures move to nearby cells
  2. Combat - Males fight each other, wolves attack humans, dogs attack wolves
  3. Eating - Humans eat fruits and mushrooms
  4. Reproduction - Males and females may reproduce
  5. Death - Creatures age and may die naturally
  6. Birth - Pregnant females give birth
  7. Plant Spawning - New fruits and mushrooms appear

⚡ Important Rules

  • All creatures complete each phase before the next phase begins
  • Creatures age every round and may die of old age (Gompertz mortality)
  • When populations exceed thresholds, death rates increase (overcrowding)
  • The round counter increases after all 7 phases complete

👨 Male Humans

Males can fight each other and wolves. They can reproduce with females. Males move toward nearby fruits when hungry.

👩 Female Humans

Females can reproduce with males. After mating, females become pregnant for 3 rounds, then give birth. They have a cooldown period before they can reproduce again.

🐺 Wolves

Wolves hunt humans aggressively. They have a wider perception range than humans and move toward nearby humans. Wolves age and die naturally over time.

🐕 Dogs

Dogs protect humans by attacking wolves. They move toward nearby wolves and can counter-attack when wolves attack them. Dogs age and die naturally.

🍎 Fruits

Fruits provide energy to humans. They take 2 rounds to ripen (shown by color change). Only ripe fruits can be eaten. Humans heal when eating fruits.

🍄 Mushrooms

Mushrooms are poisonous! When humans eat mushrooms, they lose health instead of gaining it. Be careful - mushrooms look similar to fruits but are dangerous.

🌱 Plant Spawning

New fruits and mushrooms spawn randomly on empty cells each round. Fruits are more common than mushrooms.

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space - Pause/Resume simulation
  • Right Arrow (→) - Advance 1 round when paused
  • Up Arrow (↑) - Advance 5 rounds when paused
  • Down Arrow (↓) - Pause continuous simulation
  • Left Arrow (←) - Pause continuous simulation
  • Escape (Esc) - Close this modal

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Start Game - Begin simulation with random entities
  • Pause/Resume - Toggle continuous execution
  • Step - Execute one round manually
  • Run - Resume continuous execution
  • Reset - Clear board and restart
  • Finish Game - End simulation and return to start
  • Speed - Choose Slow/Medium/Fast execution speed
